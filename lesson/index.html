For teachers: what the model means

Circadian rhythms persist without daily light cues, though their timing can drift. The SCN helps coordinate clocks in the body. Gene–protein feedback contributes to biological timing.

The cave, brain, neurons and cell are an artistic journey across scales. Shapes, signal paths, pulse speeds and protein counts are illustrative. The source’s CLK/PER shorthand simplifies a mechanism involving several molecular partners; this is not an anatomical or biochemical simulation.
